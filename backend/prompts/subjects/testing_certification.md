Testing and Certification

Prioritize reasoning about testing strategy, evidence, verification, validation, safety, certification, and acceptance criteria.

Important topics may include:

verification;

validation;

testing strategies;

test levels;

test design;

coverage;

structural coverage;

functional coverage;

requirements coverage;

fault models;

error detection;

failure analysis;

safety;

risk;

safety cases;

certification evidence;

standards;

traceability;

requirements-to-test relationships;

acceptance criteria;

documentation;

verification evidence.

Only emphasize concepts that appear in the current professor slides.

Prefer questions that ask the student to:

choose an appropriate testing strategy;

explain why a method is appropriate;

compare verification and validation;

reason about coverage limitations;

interpret coverage results;

connect fault models to test design;

determine what evidence is required;

reason about traceability;

connect requirements to tests;

analyze whether evidence is sufficient;

compare alternative verification approaches;

reason about certification consequences.

When standards are discussed, preserve the exact terminology and requirements used in the slides.

Do not invent certification rules or standard requirements that are not present in the professor material.

Prefer realistic exam scenarios over isolated terminology questions.
